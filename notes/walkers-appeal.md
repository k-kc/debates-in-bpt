# David Walker’s Appeal (1829, 1830, 1830)

### Quick Notes
- Three states passed laws making it illegal to teach any Black person (free or enslaved) to read or write
- Smuggled into coastal cities in the lining of Black sailor’s coats
- Connected with Nat Turner’s Rebellion (1831) 
- NC 1830 Leglislative Session ⅓ of bills passed pertain to limiting the freedoms of Blacks
- First public call for immediate abolition
- Garrison in the 2nd edition of The Liberator denounces the Appeal’s call to arms 
- Uses Jeffersonian language of “right to revolution” from Declaration of Independence
